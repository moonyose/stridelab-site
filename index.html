StrideLab

Sprint-video analysis for iPhone and iPad. Film a start, a run on the straight or the bend, and see it in slow motion with a skeleton, measured angles and coaching tips on the frames they're about.

  • Record at 120 fps in the app, or import a video, including an iPhone Slo-mo's original file.
  • Recognises starts, strides on the straight and the bend, and asks when it isn't sure.
  • Coach mode stops on each moment worth fixing, with the tip pointing at the body.
  • Everything stays on your device: no account, no ads, no analytics.
